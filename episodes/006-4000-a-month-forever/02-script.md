# 02 — Script: Shorts "This Number Pays You $4,000 a Month"

- **Format:** YouTube Shorts, vertical 1080×1920, target 45–50 s. Same style as ep005: second person, fast beats, a gag and a sound on every action.
- **Word count:** 118 words (≈ 44 s voice at 2.7 words/s + pauses + 1 s tail ≈ 48 s).
- **Promise kept:** the hook's number is real ($1.2M × 4% = $48,000/yr = $4,000/mo). "Forever" is broken on screen: the rule was built for about 30 years [Bengen 1994; Trinity 1998].
- **Facts (01-research.md):** 4% first-year withdrawal, adjusted for inflation, lasted at least 30 years in past U.S. data [Bengen 1994; Trinity]. $457/mo for 40 years at 7%/yr → $1.2M; own money $219,444, growth $980,556 [own calc, 01-research.md §3]. S&P 500 ≈ 6.8%/yr after inflation, 1928–2025 [Damodaran + FRED CPI]. Not guaranteed; crashes, taxes, inflation [§5].

## Narration (one line = one scene)

1. This number pays you four thousand dollars a month. Forever.
2. The number? One point two million dollars.
3. Here's the trick. Invest it, and take out just four percent a year.
4. That's forty-eight thousand a year. Four thousand a month.
5. In past U.S. markets, the pile usually kept going for thirty years or more.
6. But forever? Not quite. It was built to last about thirty years.
7. And crashes, taxes, and inflation can bite.
8. So how do you get one point two million? Not by luck.
9. About four hundred sixty dollars a month, invested for forty years.
10. At seven percent a year after inflation. Not guaranteed.
11. You put in two hundred twenty thousand. Compounding adds almost a million.
12. Time does the heavy lifting.
