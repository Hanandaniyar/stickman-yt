## 1. Will a Raise Push You Into a Higher Tax Bracket? (The Truth)
*(61 chars)*. Alternatives: "How Tax Brackets Actually Work (Most People Get It Wrong)" (56) / "Your Tax Bracket Isn't Your Tax Rate. Here's Why" (48)

**Angle.** Lots of people believe "a raise into the next bracket means all my income gets taxed more." We bust that with one visual: **income is water poured into a row of buckets** (or a staircase). Each bucket has its own tax rate, and only the water in the 22% bucket pays 22%. Then we show **marginal vs. effective rate** with real 2027 numbers, and **why brackets move every year** (indexed to inflation, so they don't silently tax you more). Physics flavor: filling buckets in order, like overflow in connected vessels. No tax-avoidance tips and no product names.

**Why US viewers care.** It covers raises, overtime, side gigs, and whether to accept a promotion. It also matches the W-4 and tax-season search spikes (Jan–Apr 2027) and the "what are the new brackets" news cycle right now.

| Criterion | Score | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| CPM | 5 | Taxes are among the highest-paying ad categories (tax software, accountants, fintech). |
| Search interest | 5 | "tax brackets 2027" is spiking after the Nov 5 IRS release. "do I get taxed more if I get a raise" is a perennial query that peaks again in tax season. |
| Stickman fit | 5 | Buckets filling, a staircase, coins dropping into labeled jars. One clear idea per scene. |
| Ad safety | 5 | Purely educational and nonpartisan. Avoid commenting on the law's name or politics. |

**Key facts**
1. IRS released the tax year 2027 inflation adjustments on **Nov 5, 2026** (IR-2026-133, Rev. Proc. 2026-35). The rates stay at 10/12/22/24/32/35/37%. For single filers, 22% applies over **$52,025**, 24% over **$109,125**, and 37% over **$661,375**. For married filing jointly, 22% applies over **$104,050**.
   Source: https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/irs-releases-tax-inflation-adjustments-for-tax-year-2027-taxpayers-continue-to-benefit-from-the-working-families-tax-cuts
2. 2027 standard deduction: **$16,600** single, **$33,200** married filing jointly (up $1,000), **$24,925** head of household (same IRS release).
3. The 2026 comparison (IR-2025-103, Oct 9, 2025): single 22% bracket began over **$50,400**, standard deduction **$16,100** single / **$32,200** MFJ. So the 22% threshold moved up $1,625 for 2027, which is inflation indexing in action.
   Source: https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/irs-releases-tax-inflation-adjustments-for-tax-year-2026-including-amendments-from-the-one-big-beautiful-bill
4. Derived (our arithmetic, 2027 single, taxable income): a raise from $51,500 to $52,500 crosses into 22%. The extra tax is $525×12% + $475×22% ≈ **$167.50**, so the raise is still worth about **$832** before payroll and state tax. Total federal income tax on $52,500 ≈ **$6,092**, an **effective rate ≈ 11.6%**, not 22%.

## 2. Why the Minimum Payment Keeps You in Credit Card Debt
*(55 chars)*. Alternatives: "Compound Interest in Reverse: The Credit Card Trap" (49) / "How a $5,000 Credit Card Balance Turns Into $14,500" (51)

**Angle.** Episode 001 was about money standing still. This one is **compound interest running backwards**. A stickman shovels sand out of a hole while a hose (interest) keeps pouring it back in, and the minimum payment barely beats the hose. We explain APR → monthly rate, why the minimum is designed to be small, and why the payment amount matters more than anything else. Mechanism only: no card brands, no debt-relief pitches.

**Why US viewers care.** Most households carry or have carried a card balance. Holiday spending is starting right now (November–December), and the new NY Fed data puts it in the news.

| Criterion | Score | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| CPM | 5 | Credit, debt consolidation, and banking advertisers pay top rates. |
| Search interest | 5 | "how to pay off credit card debt" and "credit card interest calculator" are evergreen. Seasonal spike in Dec–Jan. |
| Stickman fit | 5 | Hole and hose, snowball, treadmill. Very visual. |
| Ad safety | 4 | Safe, but debt topics can attract low-quality debt-relief ads. Avoid shaming language. |

**Key facts**
1. NY Fed Household Debt and Credit Report, **Nov 10, 2026**: total household debt rose $334B (1.8%) in Q3 2026 to **$19.1 trillion**, credit card balances rose $22B to **$1.29 trillion**, and **5.0%** of outstanding debt was in some stage of delinquency (up 0.3 pp).
   Source: https://www.newyorkfed.org/newsevents/news/research/2026/20261110
2. Federal Reserve G.19 (released **Nov 6, 2026**, Q3 2026 preliminary): average credit card APR is **21.19%** on all accounts and **22.36%** on accounts assessed interest.
   Source: https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g19/current/default.htm
3. Derived (our arithmetic): a $5,000 balance at 22.36% APR accrues about **$93/month** in interest at the start. Paying a fixed **$100/month** takes about **146 months (~12 years)** and about **$9,535 in interest**. Paying **$200/month** takes about **34 months** and about **$1,793 in interest**.

## 3. Why Mortgage Rates Don't Follow the Fed
*(39 chars)*. Alternatives: "The Fed Didn't Raise Rates. So Why Is My Mortgage 7%?" (52) / "Who Actually Sets Your Mortgage Rate?" (37)

**Angle.** Most people think "the Fed sets mortgage rates." In fact the Fed sets an **overnight** rate, while 30-year mortgages track the **10-year Treasury** plus a spread. Stickman visual: two ropes. The Fed's short rope is tied to a little boat, and the mortgage is a big ship anchored to a long chain (bond market expectations about inflation for years ahead). We explain the spread and why a $400k loan's payment swings by hundreds of dollars. Stay neutral on the Fed and its people: no names, no opinions on policy.

**Why US viewers care.** Buying vs. renting, refinancing, and home affordability. Rates are about 1 point higher than a year ago.

| Criterion | Score | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| CPM | 5 | Mortgage and real-estate lenders are top-tier advertisers. |
| Search interest | 4 | "mortgage rates" is huge, but the explainer query ("why mortgage rates don't follow the Fed") is narrower. Spikes around each FOMC meeting (next: Dec 2026). |
| Stickman fit | 4 | Boat and ship, ropes, a seesaw for bond prices vs. yields. Slightly more abstract than #1–2. |
| Ad safety | 4 | Safe if we avoid political framing of the Fed. Current Fed leadership is politically charged, so don't name anyone. |

**Key facts**
1. FOMC, **Oct 28, 2026**: "The Committee decided to maintain the target range for the federal funds rate at 3-3/4 to 4 percent." The vote was 10–2, and both dissenters preferred a 1/4-point hike. "Inflation remains elevated."
   Source: https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20261028a.htm
2. Freddie Mac PMMS, **Nov 5, 2026**: 30-year fixed averaged **7.18%** (a year ago: 6.22%). 15-year fixed was **6.57%** (a year ago: 5.50%). A new survey comes out every Thursday, so refresh before scripting.
   Source: https://www.freddiemac.com/pmms
3. 10-year Treasury yield (FRED DGS10): **4.88%** on **Nov 9, 2026** (4.95% on Nov 3). Mortgage minus 10-year ≈ 2.3 points of spread.
   Source: https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/DGS10
4. Derived (our arithmetic): principal and interest on a $400,000 30-year loan is about **$2,710/mo at 7.18%** vs. about **$2,455/mo at 6.22%**. That's roughly **$255/month**, or about **$91,700 over 30 years**.

## 4. The 401(k) Match: The Only Guaranteed Return You'll Get
*(57 chars)*. Alternatives: "How a 401(k) Actually Works, Explained With Stickmen" (51) / "Are You Leaving Free Money on the Table at Work?" (48)

**Angle.** Skip the "pick funds" part (no investment advice). Focus on **plumbing**: pre-tax money in, your employer's match as an instant 50–100% boost, contribution limits, and compounding over decades. Visual: a stickman puts $1 into a pipe, the employer adds $0.50, and a snowball grows downhill. Neutral on specific funds; mention only that the match typically requires you to contribute.

**Why US viewers care.** Most private-sector workers have access to a plan. November–December is open-enrollment season, and the IRS will publish the 2027 limits imminently (the 2026 limits came out Nov 13, 2025).

| Criterion | Score | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| CPM | 5 | Retirement and brokerage advertisers pay very well. |
| Search interest | 5 | "401k contribution limit 2027" will spike the day the IRS announces. "how does a 401k work" is evergreen. |
| Stickman fit | 4 | Pipes and snowball work well, but tax treatment (traditional vs. Roth) needs careful simplification. |
| Ad safety | 5 | Educational, no product picks. |

**Key facts**
1. 2026 limits (IRS IR-2025-111, **Nov 13, 2025**): employee deferral **$24,500** (up from $23,500), age-50+ catch-up **$8,000**, ages 60–63 catch-up **$11,250**, IRA **$7,500**.
   Source: https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/401k-limit-increases-to-24500-for-2026-ira-limit-increases-to-7500
2. 2027 limits are **not yet official** as of Nov 12, 2026. Milliman's final forecast projects **$25,500**. Wait for the IRS notice before scripting numbers.
   Source: https://www.milliman.com/en/insight/2027-irs-limits-forecast-final-estimates
3. Vanguard *How America Saves 2026* (**June 16, 2026**): participation hit a record **86%**, the average savings rate was **12.1%**, and employer contributions hit a record **4.7%**. Nearly two-thirds of plans default participants at 4% or higher.
   Source: https://corporate.vanguard.com/content/corporatesite/us/en/corp/who-we-are/pressroom/press-release-vanguards-twenty-fifth-how-america-saves-reveals-quiet-retirement-revolution-061626.html
4. Derived (illustrative): on a $60,000 salary with a "50% up to 6%" match, contributing 6% ($3,600) brings in $1,800 from the employer. That's an instant 50% on that money before any market return.

## 5. Is AI Making Your Electric Bill Higher? The Physics of Power
*(61 chars)*. Alternatives: "Why AI Needs So Much Electricity (and Who Pays)" (46) / "The Hidden Cost of Every AI Prompt" (34)

**Angle.** Combines tech and physics: why chips turn electricity into heat, why data centers need cooling, and how the grid works like a shared pipe whose **capacity** (not just energy) costs money. Then the money link: when demand outruns supply, capacity prices rise and utilities spread grid costs across all customers. We present both sides (prices also depend on gas, aging grid, and rate design) to stay neutral.

**Why US viewers care.** Electric bills are rising faster than overall inflation in some regions. AI is in everyone's daily life, and the topic is a frequent local-news story.

| Criterion | Score | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| CPM | 4 | Tech plus energy/home is solid, a bit below pure personal finance. |
| Search interest | 4 | "data centers electric bill" is trending but more regional (PJM states, Texas, Virginia). |
| Stickman fit | 5 | Hot chips, fans, a grid as water pipes, a "capacity auction" as a crowded bridge. Great for physics visuals. |
| Ad safety | 4 | Contested and sometimes politicized (state utility fights). Present the evidence neutrally and avoid blaming companies or parties. |

**Key facts**
1. BLS CPI, September 2026 (released **Oct 14, 2026**): the electricity index rose **4.4%** over 12 months, and utility (piped) gas service rose **5.0%**.
   Source: https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/cpi_10142026.htm
2. DOE/LBNL (**Dec 20, 2024**): data centers used about **4.4%** of US electricity (176 TWh) in 2023, projected at **6.7%–12%** by 2028.
   Source: https://www.energy.gov/articles/doe-releases-new-report-evaluating-increase-electricity-demand-data-centers
3. EIA STEO (May 12, 2026, via Utility Dive, May 15, 2026): average residential price is about **18.2¢/kWh** in 2026, nearly 5% higher than 2025. Commercial use (which includes data centers) is expected to **surpass residential for the first time on record in 2027**. Check the latest STEO edition before scripting.
   Source: https://www.utilitydive.com/news/data-centers-electricity-residential-rates-eia/820342/
4. PJM 2028/29 capacity auction (results **Jul 14, 2026**): cleared at the **$325/MW-day cap** across the footprint (about $555 without the cap), **~6.8 GW short** of the reliability target. PJM cited a roughly 2 GW forecast increase driven largely by data centers.
   Source: https://www.utilitydive.com/news/pjm-capacity-auction-price-cap-reserve-shortfall-grows/825282/
