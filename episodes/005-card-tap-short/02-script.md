# 02 — Script: Shorts "You Just Want a Coffee"

- **Format:** YouTube Shorts, vertical 1080×1920, target 45–50 s.
- **Word count:** 120 words (≈ 45 s of voice at the ep004 pace of 2.7 words/s, plus pauses and a 1 s tail ≈ 48 s).
- **Style:** second person, "You want X? Easy… but…" chain. Each beat adds one new piece to a growing machine, and the last beat shows the whole machine. Fast cuts (one scene ≈ 3–5 s), big pops, sight gags, sound on every action.
- **Facts (from ep004, already verified):** authorization in seconds via processor → card network → issuer [S1][S4]; "approved" is a promise, settlement takes 1–3 business days and fees come off first [S1][S6]; 1.59% average → about 8 cents on $5 [S5][D1]; $198.25 billion in U.S. merchant fees in 2025 [S5]; stores charge one price for everyone, so the cost is in the price [S4].

## Narration (one line = one scene)

1. You want coffee. No cash. Easy. Just tap your card.
2. Beep. Done? Nope. The café has no idea who you are.
3. So it calls a processor. The café's mail room.
4. The processor sends it down the card network, a giant highway between banks.
5. Last stop: your bank. Is the card real? Is there money? Fraud?
6. All good. Approved! In about two seconds.
7. Paid now? Still nope. Approved is just a promise.
8. The real money moves that night, and lands one to three days later.
9. Minus fees. On a five-dollar coffee, about eight cents.
10. Tiny? Across America, that's 198 billion dollars a year.
11. And who pays it? Everyone. Even cash buyers. It's baked into the price.
12. The full trip is on our channel.

## Visual ideas for Udun (not narrated)
- 1: stickman at the café counter, pats empty pockets (moths fly out), pulls out a glowing card. 
- 2: beep, then a record-scratch freeze; the barista squints, "WHO ARE YOU?" bubble.
- 3: a tiny envelope shoots out of the terminal into a mail room; a stickman clerk stamps it.
- 4: envelope rides a car down the highway, zooming past bank buildings.
- 5: grand bank hall, a stern clerk with a magnifying glass; three checkboxes tick one by one.
- 6: giant green APPROVED stamp slams down, confetti, stopwatch shows 2 s.
- 7: stamp cracks: "PROMISE ≠ PAYMENT".
- 8: night sky, moon, money trucks crawl; calendar flips 1, 2, 3.
- 9: $5 bill gets sliced like a pizza, a tiny 8¢ slice flies away.
- 10: the slice multiplies into a mountain of coins: "$198 BILLION".
- 11: price tag grows; a cash customer looks shocked at the price.
- 12: the whole machine (café → mail room → highway → bank) in one zoom-out; channel logo.
